🗺️ Skye's Adventure Map

For teachers: why we designed it this way
Audience
4th–5th grade, ages 9–11, CEFR A1
Skill
Listening, with vocabulary support
Goal
Understand weather words and choose the right clothes.
Autonomy
Learners choose their land, their help (text or slow audio) and their goal.
Practice
Word Treasures, Rainbow Rescue and Play Park.
Assessment
Stars, XP, badges and a progress bar.
Reflection
Star Map: faces, words to try again, a goal and writing.
Order
Words first, then listening, then games, then reflection.
Colors
Each mission has its own color, so children know where they are.
Sound
Optional music and sounds, with mute buttons.
Cognition
Brain Power tips: attention (focus your ears), memory (say it 3 times, picture + word) and strategies (try again).
Metacognition
Plan: a star goal before the quest. Monitor: feeling faces after each mission. Reflect: Star Map.
AI as tutor
Robot Tutor Lab: three rules (Ask, Check, Think), an example tutor chat, a catch-the-mistake game and safe prompts to copy.
Story
A broken Weather Machine gives a reason to learn the topic.
